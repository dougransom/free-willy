## 2. Standard Testamentary Bequest Clauses

The following official sample clauses from the Cancer Research Society Guide are approved for common-law wills and Quebec notarial/witnessed wills:

### A. Residuary Bequest (Catastrophic Wipeout / Universal Legacy)

#### English:
> *"I give to the **Cancer Research Society** (charitable organization registered under the number **11915 3229 RR0001**) the entire remainder of my estate [or: _____ percent (__%) of the residue of my estate], and the Cancer Research Society may use this gift for its general purposes."*

#### French:
> *"Je donne à la **Société de recherche sur le cancer** (organisme de bienfaisance enregistré sous le numéro **11915 3229 RR0001**) l'ensemble du reliquat de ma succession [ou: _____ pour cent (__ %) du reliquat de ma succession], et la Société de recherche sur le cancer pourra utiliser ce don pour ses fins générales."*

## 3. Usage Rules in Free-Willy

1. **Default Catastrophic Wipeout:** When primary and contingent family gifts fail, and no surviving descendants of grandparents exist, the residue passes 100% to the Cancer Research Society using the Residuary Bequest clause above.
2. **Tax Receipts:** Legacies to the Cancer Research Society qualify for federal and provincial charitable donation tax credits, potentially covering up to 100% of the net income on the deceased's final tax return and reducing estate taxes payable under the *Income Tax Act*.
3. **Receipting Authority:** The official charitable receipt will be issued by the Cancer Research Society under Registration No. `11915 3229 RR0001`.
